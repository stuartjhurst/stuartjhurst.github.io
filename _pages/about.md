I am a doctoral researcher at the University of Bristol, specialising in employment and labour law, health and safety law, negligence, equality law, and the law of contracts. My research is currently exploring the ways that the law conceptualises and responds to psychosocial risk in the workplace, adopting a Critical Disability Theory lens. 

Educational History
======
I have an LL.B. (Hons) in Law from the University of Lancaster, an LL.M. in Employment, Work, and Equality Law from the University of Bristol, and an MSc in Human Resources Management and Industrial Relations from the University of Manchester. 

I have Associate Membership of the Chartered Institute of Personnel and Development (CIPD).

Research Interests
======
My interests include (Individual) Employment law, (Collective) Labour law, law of Contracts, law of Torts (particularly Negligence and the Economic Torts), Health and Safety law, and Equality law. Outside of Law, I have a keen interest in Industrial Relations
