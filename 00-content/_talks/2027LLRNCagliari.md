Participating in the 8th Labour Law Research Network (LLRN) Conference, an international academic conference bringing together labour law scholars from across the world to discuss contemporary challenges and developments in the law of work. The conference explores key themes including solidarity, equality, corporate accountability, transparency and security, with particular emphasis on comparative, interdisciplinary and transnational perspectives. It provides a forum for examining the impact of digitalisation, artificial intelligence, ecological change and geopolitical developments on labour law and employment relations, while fostering international research collaboration and dialogue across different legal traditions, with particular attention to perspectives from Africa and the Global South.
